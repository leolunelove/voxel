# Voxel — Block 07

A local, single-player prison work-yard game. A mine, forest, melee yard, archery room and an entrance to newly generated dungeons.

## Play

Double-click Play Voxel.command on macOS. It starts a local server on http://127.0.0.1:4317. Keep its terminal open. Alternatively, run python3 -m http.server 4317 --bind 127.0.0.1 from this folder.

WASD move; mouse look (right-drag in the embedded browser); hold LMB to mine, cut trunk blocks or attack; arrow keys also look; Space jump; Shift sprint; E Exchange; M plots; I cargo; R yard (forfeits unbanked monster loot); Escape pause. The correct tool equips automatically. Tab opens/closes the skill book and pauses play; Escape also closes it. Archery: hold to draw, release to shoot. E at the west lobby rune enters a fresh dungeon; E at its final seal completes a cleared run.

## Current slice — build 44

- Sword and bow forging: four visually distinct tiers gated at Attack/Archery levels 1,2,4,7. Upgrades cost90/340/1100credits, equip permanently and survive defeat/reload. Swords gain damage; bows gain draw speed, projectile speed and damage. Q switches between them during hunts or dungeon runs. Ranged monster kills award Archery XP and risky loot.
- Bow has a forward-facing aim pose at every draw amount, with string/nock pull, limb flex, release recoil, brief empty nock and a dedicated release sound.
- Permanent east Archery Range with a solid bow, drawing string, real swept-collision arrows, bullseye XP and saved Archery progression.
- West Underblock lobby: E generates a new four- or five-room dungeon each run, with connected corridors, room encounters, route guidance and a final exit seal. Clear every enemy and reach the seal for Dungeoneering XP and banked loot. R abandons with no completion XP and forfeits loot. Unfinished instances reset on reload.
- Tab skill book: Mining, Woodcutting, Attack, Archery and Dungeoneering levels, XP progress and total level. Work XP derives from saved counts (4 per block,12 per felled tree); all existing progress is retained. The book pauses combat and movement.
- Old Exercise Yard through the south gate: two monster types, 100 HP, Attack XP and levels, sword auto-equip, raised-arm attack windups, warning rings, backstep/jump dodging, hit stagger/knockback, falling defeats and respawns.
- Walk out to bank monster rewards. Defeat, emergency retreat or reload forfeits all unbanked monster loot; ore, timber, tools, banked credits and Attack XP remain safe. Heal outside the hunting yard. Pausing freezes combat.
- One compact yard with a mine and a forest, rather than separate mine destinations.
- Three mine stages expand the same excavation, deepen it and reveal richer ores.
- Forest opens at level 2, after 20 mined blocks. Three farm stages grow from 3 to 6 to 10 trees.
- Two trunk blocks must break before whole-tree felling; magnetic timber, visible sapling regrowth and four axe tiers.
- Six pickaxe tiers, nine resources, 96-slot starter cargo and ten satchel expansions.
- Solid 3D pickaxe, axe and sword, with contact-driven strikes.
- Original local Voxel Pixel font, beveled stone/wood menus, matching tool icons, selected-tool hotbar and segmented meters.
- Plot and tool upgrades share the Exchange. The HUD points to the cheapest available next upgrade.
- Automatic mine replenishment with a five-second warning; stairs provide a walking exit.
- Local save migration retains old credits, tools, satchels and cargo, converting mine permits into plot stages.

No installation, CDN, cloud account or multiplayer is required. Three.js is vendored. Sound is procedural. Terrain and tree edits replenish on reload; permanent purchased upgrades remain saved.

## Verification

Run node --test tests/*.test.js. The current suite has 68 passing tests. See docs/COMPACT_YARD.md and docs/VERIFICATION.md for checks and limits.

tests/camp-preview.html is a read-only visual comparison of the three expansion stages. It does not access gameplay saves.

Voxel Pixel is an original bitmap-outline font generated by tools/build-pixel-font.py using the Python standard library; its font file is bundled locally.

### Build 54 — Prison shifts, stamina and cell rest
A 12-minute active-play shift moves from 06:00 through daylight, dusk and night; at midnight time waits for voluntary sleep. Menus/pause freeze the shift. Yard sunlight, ambient light and fog follow the clock while warm lamps remain readable; dungeon lighting stays stable. Mining/chopping and active combat drain saved stamina; walking and jumping home are free. Below 20 stamina work power is 75 percent, at zero 50 percent; movement and combat remain available. A compact lit cell beside the yard has a physical doorway, bed, barred window and signs. E inside the cell ends the shift, restores health/stamina, advances the day and shows gross credits earned, newly gathered resource counts, all five skill XP gains and highlighted level-ups. Earnings already credited by sale/banking are never paid again. Shift state and baseline XP persist; sleep commits the new morning before showing its report, so reload cannot duplicate totals. Browser completed mining, sale, cell sleep and wake: 412 CR earnings, 6 slate/4 copper/3 iron, +12 Mining XP and level 3→4; reload retained shift 3, 529 credits and level 4. All 92 tests pass, including cell collision/reachability and shift validation. Full real-time night pacing and fatigue balance still need human playtesting.
