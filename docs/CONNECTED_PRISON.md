# Block 07 / Connected prison

The central yard is the starting point. Deepwell lies west through its starting-permit gate. The service road heads north: Ironworks branches west at its first junction; Gilded Fault and Ember Hollow branch west/east at the far junction. The timber yard is east of the central yard.

Each mine has its own physical lifting gate. Mine A starts open. B, C and D use existing sequential permits, equipment requirements and prices. Purchasing a permit opens the gate permanently; Show route gives directions and distance rather than teleporting. Moving between wings preserves mined blocks until reload or manual refill. Permanent half-block stairs lead from bedrock to the entrance in each wing. Walking handles these steps automatically; one-block obstacles still require jumping.

Prison level 2 is earned at 20 ore blocks mined. Timber does not contribute to this count. Level 2 opens the timber gate permanently, including for existing saves that have already mined 20 blocks. Hold the normal mining control on trunks to collect three timber fragments per block. Timber follows the magnetic pickup/capacity rules and sells for 4 credits per fragment. Fully harvested trees regrow after 35 active seconds; regrowth waits if the player occupies the trunk.

WASD, sprint, jump and the restrained pickaxe remain available throughout. E opens the Exchange. R returns to the central yard if needed. Menu pause stops harvesting, pickups, regrowth and gate animation.

Save schema 3 adds timber to the inventory. Schemas 1 and 2 migrate without losing ore, credits, equipment or permits. As before, terrain excavations and tree damage are session state rather than saved voxel edits. The game remains local and single player.
