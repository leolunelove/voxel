# Build 85 — Strike the stone. Stamp the record.

Pickaxe mining commits a fixed amount of work on each actual impact, rather than accumulating damage invisibly between swings. Worn iron needs two contacts for starter slate; tempered steel needs one. Better picks also recover faster. Hold-to-mine remains the control. Release, pause and retargeting discard partial work. Other tool and combat rules retain their existing behavior.

Each block impact briefly arrests only the viewmodel, followed by sharper pitch recoil. Contact pulses the reticle; fracture stages advance on the blows. Material-colored chips have a larger break burst and a bounded lifetime/count. Ore bursts outward before a staggered magnetic return. Short material-specific impact recipes now have a fuller low-frequency body and a separate crumble tail; pickup tones are rate-limited to avoid a stack of simultaneous beeps. Resource counts, reservation/snapshot safety and ore values are unchanged.

The menu is an animated Block 07 admission record with barred insignia, numbered controls, a registered tool field and an intake/clearance stamp. New arrivals and returning prisoners receive different copy and record states. The original same-camera Begin/Resume handoff remains. Reduced motion disables the paper motion.

Validation: 201 tests pass, including no work between contacts, contact count differences for the first upgrade, retarget/release reset, pickups and save snapshots. Browser-played the menu, Begin, processing, tool issue, Cell 07, mining, jumping, collection and reloading. A 10-item Slate/Copper haul survived reload, sold once for 32 credits and persisted after another reload. Sound synthesis was exercised by the game; subjective listening and player feel still need the user's review.
