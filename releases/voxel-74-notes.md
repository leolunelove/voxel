# Voxel 74 — Iron & Ember architecture materials

Prison buildings and props now share a restrained surface treatment: charcoal stone courses with chipped mortar and small cracks; worn concrete slabs; iron seams, rivets and scuffs; chipped rust-red cell-door paint; and grain, joints and knots on timber posts, benches and crates. The existing material colors stay in control of the Iron & Ember palette. Large walls and small props use a consistent world-space texture size instead of stretching one image over the entire object. Stone top faces use a separate concrete tile, timber top faces use plank detail, and bright route markings stay legible.

The treatment uses one deterministic 256×128 RGBA atlas (128 KiB before mipmaps), with one texture lookup per fragment. Mixed-material instance batches carry a small per-instance style attribute; their instance counts, transforms, colors and draw calls remain intact. Existing ore, bark, foliage and paving maps bypass the architecture shader, as do transparent glass and emissive fixtures. Bedding keeps its plain cloth materials. The cell sign moved above the barred window, and the redundant mine header moved to an interior wall so the main MINE entrance sign is unobstructed.

Validation: 149 tests passed and the static release verified 61 files / 2.25 MiB. Browser comparison of the same courtyard view at pixel ratio 1.5 measured detail OFF: 8.38ms mean / 8.9ms p95 / 2.64ms render submission; detail ON: 8.33ms mean / 9.0ms p95 / 2.79ms render submission. Both used 102 draw calls and 49,112 triangles. This local comparison showed no meaningful frame-pacing regression; it does not guarantee a frame rate on other hardware.

Visual review covered courtyard masonry, Cell 07, the mine entrance and exchange props in daylight and at night. The playable browser check reached the cell rest prompt through the open doorway, confirmed solid wall/prop collision, walked to the mine, broke blocks, jumped and opened the exchange. Mining increased the saved haul from 1,618 to 1,860 CR. No local browser console errors were captured. Existing progression, day cycle and combat regression checks passed; no save schema changed.

The visual/performance study is tests/architecture-preview.html, excluded from the production build. Native mouse capture and physical low-end-device performance were not retested for this material-only update.
